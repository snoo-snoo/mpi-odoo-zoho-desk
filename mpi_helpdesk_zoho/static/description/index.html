MPI Helpdesk Zoho Desk Connector

Bidirectional Ticket Sync between Odoo Enterprise Helpdesk and a Zoho Desk Organization. One Connection per company.

  • Desk wins Ticket fields; Comments append; Public Comments stay public
  • Team Map and Department Map decide what crosses
  • Attachments with a size cap, MIME list, and direction on the Connection
  • Webhooks plus a 15-minute catch-up; buyer brings a Zoho self-client

Requires Odoo 19 Enterprise Helpdesk. Databases Zoho cannot reach fall back to cron.
